# TAKU KIMIANGA RAI

**KUTIA TUTERU**

TAKU KIMIANGA RAI IA KOE E INE  
KITE ATU AU I TO MATA MANEA  
UMUUMU ANA AU KIA PEKE KOE IAKU  
TANO ATU RAI TAKU I MANAKO  
MATAORA TIKAI TO TAUA PIRIANGA  

### REFRAIN

UA TAKI MAI KOE IAKU I TO IPUKAREA  
UA AKAMATA KOE TE AKANENEVA IAKU  
AUE  

EAHA KOE IAKU  
I PEHE RAHI KOE IAKU  
AUE  

NOATU TO KINO IAKU E INE  
KARE TAKU INANGARO IA KOE E NGARO  
KUA OTI I TE PAPAU KI MUA I TE ATUA  
NA TE MATE TAUA E TATARA  
AUE TE AKAROA IAKU NEI E
